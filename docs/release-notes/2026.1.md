## [2026.1] - 2026-11-12

The first release of Hive, which backs up your servers from a Mac, and Beekeeper, which manages it.

### Added

- Hive runs in the background from startup with its keys in the Secure Enclave.
- Incremental snapshots of server directories over rsync and SSH, browsable in Finder.
- MySQL, MariaDB and Postgres dumps, on the host or in Docker containers.
- Schedules, retention by day and month, and per-server quotas.
- Live progress, a job log and an audit log in Beekeeper.
- Email alerts for failed backups, a missing drive, changed host keys and low space.
- Pairing with a QR code or pairing code. Every connection is TLS, pinned to the Hive's key.
- A recovery key and encrypted recovery files, for bringing Hive back on a new Mac.
- Updates for both apps. Beekeeper shows when Hive needs an update.
